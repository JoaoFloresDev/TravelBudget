Travel Budget is the simple trip expense tracker that answers the only question that matters on vacation: how much can I spend today?

SET A BUDGET, SEE IT DAILY
Give each trip a total budget and your dates — the app instantly turns it into a daily allowance. Overspend on Tuesday? Wednesday adjusts automatically, so you always know exactly where you stand for the rest of the trip.

LOG AN EXPENSE IN 3 TAPS
Amount, category, done. Adding a cost takes seconds and works completely offline — on the plane, on a remote beach, on the metro. No connection needed, ever.

ANY CURRENCY, HONEST RATES
Spend in euro, yen, pesos or anything else: the exchange rate of the day is saved, dated and locked with each expense, so your totals never drift when rates change later. Every rate stays visible and editable — no mystery conversions, no stale numbers quietly rewriting your vacation cost.

KNOW WHERE THE MONEY WENT
A clean donut chart shows spending by category — food, transport, hotels, fun — plus daily stats across the whole trip. Perfect for a weekend road trip, a backpacking summer through Europe, a honeymoon or business travel with a fixed daily budget.

A WIDGET THE OTHERS DON'T HAVE
See today's remaining money without opening the app — right on your home screen or lock screen. Glance at your phone, decide, and get back to enjoying the day.

YOUR TRIPS ARE YOURS, FOREVER
- No account, no login, no cloud lock-in: everything lives on your device, so a trip never disappears behind a signup screen.
- Past trips stay fully open — your travel history is never locked away after the vacation ends.
- Export any trip to CSV for free, whenever you want, and keep your own records.

FREE AND COMPLETE
Every feature above is included. No subscription, no locked screens, no surprises — just a fast, private travel wallet for your spending.

MADE FOR EVERY KIND OF TRAVELER
Backpackers counting every euro, couples planning a honeymoon, families on holiday, digital nomads living abroad, business travelers on a per diem — anyone who wants to travel without money anxiety.

Plan the trip. Set the budget. Spend with confidence.
